YES PA INAMDAR SCHOOL LIBRARY CODE OF CONDUCT

These rules are designed to maintain an environment conducive to learning, reading, research, and smooth library operations for both students and staff.

Rules for Students & Readers

  • Silence & Decorum: Perfect silence must be maintained in the reading hall. Group discussions are strictly prohibited in quiet study zones.
  • Book Handling: Handle all books with care. Do not write, underline, mark, or fold pages of library books or periodicals.
  • Borrowing Allowance: Students may borrow books according to their eligibility limit and must present their valid library ID.
  • Timely Return: Books must be returned on or before the specified due date. Late returns will incur overdue charges.
  • Inspection before Issue: Readers should inspect books prior to leaving the circulation counter and notify staff of any pre-existing damage.
  • Personal Belongings: Bags, personal books, and eatables must be deposited at the property counter before entering the library.

Rules & Duties for Librarians

  • Accurate Circulation Records: Log all book issues, returns, and renewals immediately in the library system without delay.
  • Equal & Fair Access: Provide polite, professional assistance and equal borrowing access to all students and staff members.
  • Inventory Maintenance: Routinely check cataloging, subject categorization, and physical condition of books on shelves.
  • Overdue & Fine Enforcement: Collect and record overdue fines fairly as per official school rules, issuing valid receipts or system logs.
  • Data Security & Privacy: Protect student reading history and user credentials. Ensure routine database backups are taken.
  • Environment Control: Ensure quiet reading hours, clean environment, and proper lighting across reading sections.

Lost Book & Damage Penalty Policy

Overdue Fine

A nominal fine per day is calculated for books returned after the due date. Continued delay may restrict further borrowing privileges.

Damaged Book

If a book is damaged or torn while in a reader's possession, the reader must pay for rebinding costs or replace the book with a fresh copy.

Lost Book

In case of lost books, the borrower must replace the book with the same edition or pay the current market price of the book.
